# Unstuck: start here

Unstuck helps the moment you get stuck in a recorded lecture or a YouTube explainer. It runs in **Chrome** (not Safari).

## Install (once, 3 minutes)

1. Get the **Unstuck** folder: the `dist` folder from building it (see the README), or the zip someone sent you, unzipped. Put it somewhere it can stay (Documents, for example).
2. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions`.
3. Switch on **Developer mode** (top right).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and choose that folder.
5. Unstuck's setup page opens. Follow its three steps:
   - **Your Gemini key:** free, from Google AI Studio (the page shows how). Paste it and click Save.
   - **The camera** (optional): lets Unstuck notice when you look confused. Your face stays on your laptop.
6. Pin it: click the puzzle piece next to the address bar, then the pin next to **Unstuck**.

## Use it

- Open a YouTube explainer and click the knot in the player's control bar, or open a lecture on Panopto and press play: there Unstuck starts by itself and the knot at the top left of the video turns orange.
- When you look stuck, or rewind a bit, a small note offers help. Press **E** (or click it).
- Press **E** any time to get help with what's on screen. Ask a follow-up in the chat, or click **Simpler, please**.
- **U** switches Unstuck off and on.

## Good to know

- Chrome may warn about extensions in developer mode when it starts. That's normal for an extension that isn't from the Chrome Web Store: keep it.
- A new version: replace the folder with the new one, then click the round arrow on Unstuck in `chrome://extensions`.
- Something odd? Open an issue at https://github.com/ralphvspanje-hub/unstuck_open/issues and say what you were watching and when.
